What this is

Maeum Shorts Uploader is a personal, single-user tool that runs only on the channel owner's own Windows PC. It helps the owner of the YouTube channel 마음이 쉬어가는 이야기 ("Stories where the heart rests", @마음이쉬는이야기) publish short, original storytelling videos: retellings of traditional Korean temple legends, Bible stories, folk tales and old proverbs that comfort people in hard times.

「마음이 쉬어가는 이야기」 채널 주인이 자기 PC 에서만 쓰는 개인용 업로드 도구입니다. 옛 전설·성경 이야기·옛 지혜를 30초 쇼츠로 새로 만들어, 주인이 하나씩 확인하고 승인한 영상만 올립니다.

How it works

  1. The tool prepares a short video on the owner's PC (original script, locally generated illustrations and narration).
  2. The owner receives a private preview and watches it.
  3. Only when the owner taps "Upload" for that one video, the tool uploads it to the owner's own channel through the YouTube Data API (videos.insert) as a private video scheduled to go public at a chosen time.

The tool never uploads to any other channel. It has no other users, no sign-up and no public interface. It also reads public data (titles and view counts of public Shorts, public comments on our own channel) with an API key to see which stories help people most, and replies to public comments on our own channel (short, polite replies drafted by an AI model and posted automatically under strict safety rules; spam is ignored).
