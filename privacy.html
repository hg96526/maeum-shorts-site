Maeum Shorts Uploader · effective October 28, 2026

Maeum Shorts Uploader ("the tool") is a private, single-user tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel 마음이 쉬어가는 이야기 (@마음이쉬는이야기) on the owner's own computer. This policy explains what data the tool uses.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • Upload permission (OAuth scope youtube.upload) — granted only by the channel owner, used only to upload videos the owner has individually approved to the owner's own channel. The tool does not read private account data.
  • Public data via an API key — titles, view counts and subscriber counts of public Shorts (to see which kinds of stories people find helpful), public statistics of our own channel, and public comments posted on our own channel's videos.

How the data is used

  • To upload and schedule the owner's approved videos.
  • To choose story topics and measure how our own videos perform.
  • To show the owner new comments on our channel and prepare reply drafts. Comment text may be processed by an AI model (Anthropic Claude) only to classify the comment and draft a reply; the owner reviews every reply before posting.

The tool does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, does not show ads, and does not build profiles of viewers.

Storage and deletion

  • All data stays on the owner's computer. The OAuth token is stored in a local file and never leaves that computer.
  • Cached public statistics (titles and view counts) are kept for at most 30 days and then deleted or refreshed.
  • Collected comments (at most the latest 200) are kept only to prepare reply drafts and are deleted within 30 days.
  • To request deletion of any data about you, email us; we delete it within 7 days.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access stops all uploads immediately.
